# Voice Design Specifications: Barnaby & Pip

These prompts and acoustic profiles are formatted to send directly to Voice Design AIs (such as **ElevenLabs Voice Design**, **MiniMax Audio**, **OpenAI Voice Engine**, or **PlayHT**).

---

## 🐻 1. Barnaby the Apprentice Bear (<Audio 1>)

### 📋 Direct Voice AI Prompt (Copy & Paste)
> "A youthful, gentle male voice of a 10 to 12-year-old bear cub apprentice. Warm, round, and cuddly timbre with soft chest resonance and a cozy storybook feel. Mild, friendly, earnest, and slightly breathy with a charmingly innocent cadence. Speaks at a relaxed, thoughtful pace with genuine cheerfulness and clear diction. Gentle British storybook / Paddington-inspired warmth. Never harsh, gravelly, or gruff."

### 🎙️ Acoustic Profile & Characteristics
- **Gender / Age Category**: Boy / Young Adolescent (10–12 years old)
- **Pitch**: Mid-to-high register with a round, warm resonance (avoiding deep adult bear grunts or shrill squeaks)
- **Timbre**: Plush, velvety, slightly woolly/fluffy softness; warm acoustic body with clean low-mid warmth
- **Cadence & Rhythm**: Relaxed, earnest, unhurried, thoughtful; articulates with polite warmth and occasional gentle bear chuckles
- **Accent / Dialect**: Warm Storybook British (soft RP / light West Country undertone) or gentle rural storybook English
- **Personality Traits**: Humble, hard-working, sweet-tempered, eager to please, loyal, optimistic
- **Touchstone Inspirations**: Paddington Bear (Ben Whishaw), classic Winnie the Pooh, spirited young Studio Ghibli protagonists (e.g., Pazu from *Castle in the Sky*)

### 🗣️ Audition / Generation Test Lines
- *"Good morning, Pip! Ready to make the star-berry pie?"*
- *"Now where did that big golden star-berry roll off to?"*
- *"Easy does it... almost got it!"*
- *"We did it, Pip! The prettiest star-berry pie in the whole valley!"*

## 🐦 2. Pip the Magical Hummingbird Finch (<Audio 2>)

### 📋 Direct Voice AI Prompt (Copy & Paste)
> "A bright, high-pitched, and delightfully chirpy animated critter voice. Light, airy, and bell-like timbre with a musical, whistling lilt. Fast, bouncy, and energetic delivery filled with childlike curiosity and cheerful excitement. Clear, articulate enunciation woven with subtle melodic twitters and joyful pitch rises. Playful, friendly, and magical fairy/mascot companion archetype. High energy, sweet, and sunny."

### 🎙️ Acoustic Profile & Characteristics
- **Gender / Age Category**: Youthful / Ageless magical companion (bright childlike register)
- **Pitch**: High register; soprano lilt with agile melodic contour and frequent upward inflections
- **Timbre**: Feather-light, sparkling, crystalline, buoyant; clean, crisp consonants without harsh sibilance
- **Cadence & Rhythm**: Fast, fluttery, energetic, dynamic; staccato bursts followed by melodic chirps
- **Accent / Dialect**: Clean, expressive Mid-Atlantic / Storybook English with bird-like musicality
- **Personality Traits**: Bouncy, encouraging, observant, excitable, affectionate, proud little helper
- **Touchstone Inspirations**: Jiminy Cricket, Flounder (*The Little Mermaid*), playful friendly anime companions (Chopper, Navi, Pabu)

### 🗣️ Audition / Generation Test Lines
- *(Excited sharp chirp)* "Found it, Barnaby! Up top!" *(happy melodic trill)*
- *(Melodic two-tone chirp)* "Look over here! On the high shelf!"
- *(Encouraging breathy cheep)* "Almost got it! Easy does it!"
- *(Ecstatic warble)* "The prettiest pie in the valley!" *(triumphant flutter)*
